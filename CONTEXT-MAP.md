# Context Map

Two deliberately separate vocabularies. The whole point of the separation is that a
1986 word carries a 1986 assumption, and importing the word silently imports the
assumption.

## Contexts

- [Habitat (historical)](./CONTEXT-HABITAT.md) — the vocabulary needed to *read the
  primary sources*. Fixed by history; we do not get a vote on these definitions.
- [Commons (2026)](./CONTEXT-COMMONS.md) — the vocabulary of the federated,
  LLM-inhabited system being designed. Under active construction.

## Relationships

- **Habitat → Commons**: one-way, by citation only. A Commons term may be *derived
  from* a Habitat term, but must be independently defined and must state what it
  drops. No term is shared by reference.
